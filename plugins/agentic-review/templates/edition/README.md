# {TITLE} — reading edition

Built {BUILT} by `ar-edition`. Rebuild it with `/agentic-review:run 7` after any change to the review; do not edit
`index.html` by hand.

## Open it

- Double-click `index.html`: it works offline, in any recent browser.
- Or use a launcher, which serves the page from `http://localhost:{PORT}` and opens it:
  `open-in-chrome.command` (macOS), `open-in-browser.sh` (Linux), `open-in-browser.bat` (Windows). Keep the window it
  opens while you read; closing it stops the local server.

## What is on the page

- Contents on the left (a bar at the top on phones), the section being read highlighted.
- Key points under the section headings and headline numbers at the top, when the review provides them.
- Hover or focus a citation to preview it; a dotted underline explains a term or an abbreviation.
- References list where each paper is cited; **BibTeX** copies an entry, and a button downloads them all.
- Appendices: the paper catalogue (with a filter) and the rigor rubric and scores.
- § next to a heading copies a link to it. The light or dark choice and the "how the links work" panel (and, with
  Zotero, the ✓ marks on passages already opened) are remembered by this browser only.
